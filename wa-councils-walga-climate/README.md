## In one paragraph

WALGA's Western Australian Local Government Declaration on Climate Change
is a commitment councils sign, not a formal climate-emergency declaration
and not the same document as WALGA's 2018 Policy Statement on Climate
Change. This project asks what, if anything, changes in a WA council's
climate policy-making after it signs the Declaration, and whether any change
looks like the outcomes reported in the climate-emergency-declaration
literature (Bush and Doyon 2025; Greenfield, Moloney and Granberg 2022) or
something weaker. No published study answers this for WALGA's Declaration
specifically, as far as the scoping search behind this project has found;
confirming that gap with a documented review is the first step, not an
assumption the rest of the project leans on.

## Scope

A systematic literature review (Objective 1) and two document-analysis arms
(Objectives 2 and 3: within-council before/after, and a cross-council
comparison group) are the realistic four-month scope. Interviews with
council officers (Objective 4) are optional, ethics-gated, and treated as a
first cut to extend later if time allows, not a requirement for this phase.
